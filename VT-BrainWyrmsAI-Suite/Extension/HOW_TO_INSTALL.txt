           VT BRAINWYRMS AI - EXTENSION INSTALLATION GUIDE
========================================================================

How to install into Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, or Brave:

1. Open your browser and enter this URL:
   chrome://extensions  (or edge://extensions)

2. In the top-right corner, turn ON "Developer mode".

3. In the top-left corner, click "Load unpacked".

4. Browse to and select this exact "Extension" folder.

5. Done! The VT BrainWyrms AI icon will appear in your browser bar.
   - Click it while on canvas.vt.edu to sync your live classes.
   - Click "Launch Web Application" inside the popup anytime to open
     your personalized study schedule and AI practice engine.
